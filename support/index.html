Frequently asked questions

Why am I not receiving push notifications?

First, confirm that notifications are enabled for TZMC Push in your device settings. Also make sure your internet connection is active and that battery optimization or focus modes are not blocking alerts.

What information should I send when reporting a problem?

Please include your device model, operating system version, app version, and a clear description of the issue. Screenshots are also helpful when available.

How long does support usually take to respond?

We aim to respond within 1–2 business days. Complex issues may require additional follow-up.

Can I contact support about feature requests?

Yes. We welcome feedback and feature suggestions. Please send your idea through the support email and include as much context as possible.
