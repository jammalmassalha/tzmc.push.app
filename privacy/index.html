Full Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

TZMC Push respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use the TZMC Push mobile application and related support services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, TZMC Push may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Basic account or profile information that you provide voluntarily
  • Device information such as device model, operating system, and app version
  • Notification-related identifiers, such as push or messaging tokens
  • Support information you send when requesting help
  • Diagnostic or technical data needed to maintain app performance and reliability

3. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the app to:

  • Provide and maintain the app’s functionality
  • Send notifications, alerts, and relevant updates
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues
  • Improve app performance, stability, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms or policies

4. Notifications

If you enable notifications, TZMC Push may use notification permissions and device messaging tokens to deliver alerts and updates. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings at any time.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when necessary to operate the app, provide technical services, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and safety, or work with service providers acting on our behalf.

6. Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, resolve support requests, maintain security, comply with law, or fulfill legitimate business needs.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

TZMC Push is not intended for children unless explicitly stated otherwise. If you believe that a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable law, please contact us so we can review and address the issue.

9. Your Choices

You may be able to control certain data practices through your device settings, app permissions, and by contacting us directly. For example, you can disable notifications in your device settings.

10. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services such as hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or push delivery providers. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date on this page. Continued use of the app after changes may indicate acceptance of the updated policy, where permitted by law.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy-related request, please contact us at support@tzmc.co.il.
